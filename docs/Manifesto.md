# HomeHub Manifesto

## Why HomeHub Exists

HomeHub exists to reduce mental effort. It does not try to show everything. It decides what matters, presents one clear mission, and keeps everything else secondary.

## The Promise

Every glance should answer:

> What deserves my attention right now?

## ADHD-Friendly by Design

1. One mission, not ten competing priorities.
2. Clear hierarchy before detail.
3. Actionable language instead of passive information.
4. Calm presentation without visual noise.
5. Encouragement after completion.

## Product Principle

> Don't show everything. Show what matters.
